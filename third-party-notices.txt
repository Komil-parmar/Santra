Santra: third-party software
============================

Santra includes the open-source components listed below. Each one keeps its own licence. The full licence
texts are in the "licenses" folder next to Santra.exe, in one subfolder per component.

Some components are under the GNU Lesser General Public License (Magick.NET and ImageMagick, TagLib#, FFmpeg).
Santra uses them as separate files that you may replace with your own builds, and you may reverse engineer
Santra as far as needed to debug such changes. Where to get their exact source code is listed with each one
below, and at https://komil-parmar.github.io/Santra/open-source.html
